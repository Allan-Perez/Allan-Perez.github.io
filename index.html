Hi, I'm Allan Perez

I am a PhD student at the University of Bristol (2024-present). My supervisors are Tim Dokchitser and Céline Maistret. Previously I did my undergraduate degree at the University of Glasgow (2019-2023), and my master's at the University of Oxford (2023-2024).

My interests lie in the area of arithmetic geometry of curves. I mainly study how curves degenerate at finite primes, with an emphasis on computing certain invariants (such as local monodromy or Tamagawa numbers) of these degenerations, given an explicit curve (e.g. a plane model). I'm also interested in the closely related subject of degeneration of abelian varieties.
